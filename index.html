Risk Assessment

C-SSRS guided screening. Complete all items before routing to a scenario.

🔗 Full C-SSRS Reference

C-SSRS Screening

Answer each question based on the client's current presentation. Questions 3–5 appear only if Question 2 is endorsed.

Ideation
Question 1 — Passive Ideation
Have you wished you were dead or wished you could go to sleep and not wake up?
Question 2 — Active Ideation
Have you actually had any thoughts about killing yourself?
If yes to Q2 — continue with Questions 3–5
Question 3 — Method
Have you been thinking about how you might do this?
Question 4 — Intent ⚠ Auto High Risk if Yes
Have you had these thoughts and had some intention of acting on them?
Question 5 — Plan ⚠ Auto High Risk if Yes
Have you started to work out or worked out the details of how to kill yourself? Did you intend to carry out this plan?
Behavior
Question 6 — Past Suicidal Behavior ⚠ Auto High Risk if recent
Have you done anything, started to do anything, or prepared to do anything to end your life?

If lifetime only: consider alongside current ideation and other risk factors before determining overall risk level.

Clinical judgment note: This screening tool supports — but does not replace — clinical judgment. Risk level should be determined by integrating C-SSRS findings with protective factors, client history, therapeutic alliance, and your full clinical assessment. When in doubt, consult.

Protective Factors

Check all that apply. Weigh alongside risk factors in overall clinical formulation.

LOW RISK

Protective Factors Noted
0 of 8

Patient Willing for Voluntary Care?
No Yes

Relevant for high-risk routing: willing → Scenario B, unwilling → Scenario C.

C-SSRS Decision Rules
Low / Monitor: Q1 or Q2 yes; Q3–Q6 all no
Moderate: Q3 yes; or Q6 lifetime only (consider full picture)
High: Q4 or Q5 yes; or Q6 within past 3 months
If Q2 = No, skip Q3–Q5. Q4, Q5, and recent Q6 are each independently sufficient for HIGH risk. Clinical judgment always governs the final determination.
